The overlooked way top creators use Trial reels

Most people use them wrong. This is what the best do.

The formula Post every video as a Trial reel in 3 variants. Same video each time. Only the text hook on screen changes. Then post the version that wins to your followers.

Why it works Text hooks make a huge difference. Tiny changes can move views by up to 34,000. You test which hook performs best before the video goes on your profile, so you only post what already works. That brings in many more followers and reaches far more people.

Hook type 1: the pain point Open with the problem people have right now, in their own words.

Hook type 2: the claim Open with the result or the statement itself, with no introduction.

Hook type 3: the unexpected one Open with something that sounds a little wrong or goes against what people believe, so they stay for the next three seconds.
