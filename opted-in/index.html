D1: You’re Losing Clients In The 10 Seconds After “It’s Too Expensive”

Preview Free: the exact 3 steps, word for word

“Your price is too high” is costing you clients

Right now, when a client says your price is too high, most women over-explain, defend their value and drop the price (or offer a payment plan) before the client has even finished their sentence. That's how you lose authority.

So here's how to fix that in 3 simple steps:

Empathy first

The dinner question

Silence

1: Empathy first

Lead with empathy, not defensiveness: “I appreciate you being honest about that. It can feel like a big investment at first.” Stay calm. Stay grounded.

2: The dinner question

Then ask: “When you go out to treat yourself to a nice dinner, do you get takeaway, or do you go to a nice restaurant for the experience and the quality?” They'll say the nice restaurant. And you say: “Of course. So why would you treat your business any differently?”

3: Silence

Then stop talking. Don't drop the price. You anchored quality against the cheaper alternatives, and they heard themselves pick quality.

That's the full process:

D3: Ask These 4 Questions Before You Ever Pitch

Preview Free script inside. Copy it for your next call.

Turn A Sales Call Into A Yes [First-Call Script]

Pitching first is dumb.

People who feel rushed into a pitch don't feel safe, and people who don't feel safe will never buy.

You don't need a better offer. You just need the right first four questions.

Here's the exact 4-step script to do exactly that:

Step 1 - Ask What Made Them Say Yes

“Before we dive in, what made you say yes to this call today?”

Why it works:

When they say it out loud, they sell themselves.

Step 2 - Find Out What Failed

“What have you already tried that hasn't worked?”

Why it works:

It shows them you're not like the last person who took their money.

Step 3 - Paint The Vision

“If these next 3 months went exactly the way you wanted, what changes?”

Why it works:

You're painting the vision before you sell it.

Step 4 - Bring The Real Objection Into The Room

“What would need to be true for you to feel fully ready to invest in support right now?”

Why it works:

It brings the real objection into the room, before you pitch.

And now you're leading the call. The best sales calls feel like therapy, not a transaction.

D4: DON’T Send “Just Checking In”

Preview Why “just checking in” gets you silence

There's no doubt you've heard “just follow up with your leads.”

Or that “just checking in” keeps the conversation warm

But the reality is, it gets you silence…

And if you keep doing it you'll waste all the time and energy that goes into every lead… just to stay stuck where you are right now

A “just checking in” message is vague, lazy and gives zero direction. It puts the emotional work back on them, to decide if they want to re-engage.

In the exact same way, the number of dead leads you bring back to a booked call is dictated by whether they feel you're leading

Which is why inside the Mastermind, I DON'T teach check-ins - I teach reasons to say yes

A check-in is built on hoping they reply

A reason to say yes is built on a specific insight and a clear next step

A check-in asks them to decide

A reason to say yes helps them decide

A check-in gets silence

A reason to say yes gets a booked call

Now let me be clear - this will not work for everyone…

If you only want to feel busy in your DMs

But...

If you reference something real they shared

Copy This Question Set To Turn A Maybe Into A Yes

You can feel the hesitation on your call

Here's how it works:

People don't change when they see the solution. They change when they see the cost of staying the same.

So instead of over-explaining and trying to convince, there's a better way, and it has 3 parts:

Part 1 - The Pause

Most female founders panic. They start over-explaining, they justify the price and they try to convince.

Women grounded in feminine sales do the opposite. They slow down.

Part 2 - The Reframe

Hesitation isn't rejection. It's unspoken uncertainty, and uncertainty disappears when the real concern is seen.

Part 3 - The Six Questions

“Can I ask what's making this feel like a maybe right now?”

“If that piece was solved, would you feel ready to move forward?”

“Is it the investment, the timing, or something else entirely?”

“Do you feel certain this is what you need, or is there something that still feels unclear?”

“If nothing changes over the next 6 months, what does that look like for you?”

“What would help you feel fully confident moving forward?”
